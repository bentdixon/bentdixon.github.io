About

I'm Benjamin, an MSc student in Cognitive Science at Edinburgh University (School of Informatics) and Research Scientist at the Language as a Biomarker Lab under Dr. Wolff. My current research focuses on developing computational analyses of speech and pairing those analyses with neural data to discover novel biomarkers of neuropsychiatric and neurodegenerative disorders. My research also involves machine learning and representation alignment. My interests include computational psychiatry, neural time series data, and information-theoretic approaches to language. Outside of academia, I want to help build technologies that are useful for disabled people.
